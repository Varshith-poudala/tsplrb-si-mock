# TSPLRB SI Prelims Full-Length & Daily Mock Test Portal (2026)
### తెలంగాణ పోలీస్ సబ్-ఇన్‌స్పెక్టర్ (SI) ప్రిలిమ్స్ పూర్తి స్థాయి ఆన్‌లైన్ మాక్ టెస్ట్ పోర్టల్ (200 మార్కులు)

A full-fledged, mobile-responsive Computer Based Test (CBT) portal designed specifically for aspirants preparing for the **Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TSPLRB) Sub-Inspector (SCT SI Civil / AR / TSSP / SPF / SFO)** preliminary examination.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Full-Length 200-Question Official Pattern Mock Test**:
   - Strictly models the complete 200-question paper of the official TSPLRB SI Preliminary Written Test (PWT).
   - **Part A: Arithmetic (50 Questions, Q1 – Q50)**: Number systems, LCM & HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance, Mensuration (2D & 3D), and Data Interpretation.
   - **Part B: Reasoning & Mental Ability (50 Questions, Q51 – Q100)**: Analogy, Classification (Odd one out), Series (Number/Letter), Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction & Distance, Order & Ranking, Sitting Arrangement, Syllogisms & Venn Diagrams, Clocks & Calendars, Non-verbal series, and Critical Reasoning.
   - **Part C: General Studies (100 Questions, Q101 – Q200)**:
     - *Current Affairs & Contemporary Events (Q101–115)*: BRICS 2024, Aditya-L1, Paris 2024 Olympics, Mahalakshmi Scheme, Nobel 2024, TG-NAB, COP28 Dubai, 106th Constitutional Amendment.
     - *General Science & Environment (Q116–130)*: Total internal reflection, audible sound, blood pH, Plaster of Paris, Vitamins, universal donor blood, hormones, Malaria, Amrabad Tiger Reserve, greenhouse gases.
     - *History of India & National Movement (Q131–145)*: Indus Valley (Lothal), Ashoka's Kalinga War, Mehrauli Iron Pillar, Alauddin Khilji, Amuktamalyada, Din-i-Ilahi, 1857 Revolt, Satyashodhak Samaj, Swadeshi, Chauri Chaura, Dandi March, Quit India, Azad Hind, Lahore 1929.
     - *Geography of India & Telangana (Q146–157)*: Zojila pass, Godavari entry at Kandakurthi, Pranahita river, Sriram Sagar (SRSP), Singareni coal fields, Black soils, Doli Gutta peak, Nagarjuna Sagar, Bhadradri forests.
     - *Indian Polity & Governance (Q158–170)*: Preamble 42nd Amendment, Article 32, Writs (Mandamus, Quo Warranto), DPSP, Presidential pardons (Art 72), Money bill certification, Rajya Sabha, CAG (Art 148), 73rd Amendment 11th Schedule, Anti-defection 10th Schedule.
     - *Indian & Telangana Economy (Q171–180)*: Repo rate, CPI inflation, GDP definition, Fiscal deficit, GST, Hyderabad pharma exports (35%), HITEC City IT cluster, Mission Kakatiya, Mission Bhagiratha, NITI Aayog.
     - *Telangana History, Culture & Arts (Q181–190)*: Satavahanas at Kotilingala, Marco Polo at Warangal, Charminar (1591), Osmania University (1917), Bathukamma, Medaram Sammakka-Saralamma Jathara, Perini Sivatandavam, Dasarathi, Kaloji Narayana Rao, Pochampally Ikat.
     - *Telangana Movement & State Formation (Q191–200)*: Operation Polo (Sept 17, 1948), 1952 City College firing, Fazal Ali Commission (SRC), Gentlemen's Agreement (1956), 1969 Khammam hunger strike, TPS 1971 elections, Article 371-D, Chidambaram Dec 9 announcement, Million March (March 10, 2011), Appointed Day (June 2, 2014).

2. **3-Hour Real CBT Countdown Timer**:
   - Exactly **180 minutes (03:00:00)** countdown.
   - Starts automatically when the candidate clicks **"START TEST / పరీక్ష ప్రారంభించండి"** on the instructions homepage.
   - Visual urgency alerts (amber at 30 minutes, urgent red blink at 5 minutes).
   - Auto-submits the exam when the timer hits zero.

3. **10 Questions Per Page Paginator**:
   - Displays strictly **10 questions per page** across 20 pages (Page 1: Q1–10 ... Page 20: Q191–200).
   - Navigation via "« Previous Page", "Next Page »", and direct page number buttons (1 to 20).

4. **Direct Question Number Jump Box**:
   - Candidates can enter any question number (1 to 200) in the top navigation bar and click **"Go"** to instantly jump to that page and smoothly scroll to the question with highlighting.

5. **Category-wise Sidebar & 200-Question Status Palette**:
   - Filter questions by **All (200)**, **Part A: Arithmetic (1–50)**, **Part B: Reasoning (51–100)**, or **Part C: General Studies (101–200)**.
   - Color-coded question status buttons:
     - 🟢 **Green**: Answered
     - 🔴 **Red**: Not Answered (visited but not selected)
     - 🟣 **Purple**: Marked for Review
     - ⚪ **Gray**: Not Visited
   - Candidates can click any of the 200 buttons to jump directly to that question.

6. **Official TSPLRB Scoring System**:
   - **+1.00 Mark** for each correct answer.
   - **-0.20 Mark (1/5th deduction)** for each incorrect answer.
   - **0.00 Mark** for unattempted questions.

7. **Instant Performance Analysis & Bilingual Solutions**:
   - Total marks, percentage score, accuracy %, and time taken.
   - 3-Section breakdown table (Arithmetic vs Reasoning vs General Studies).
   - Complete answer key and step-by-step mathematical, logical & factual explanations in both **English and Telugu**.
   - Filters to review *All*, *Correct*, *Incorrect*, or *Unattempted* questions.

8. **WhatsApp Sharing Integration**:
   - Header **"Share Test"** button to share the mock test link with friends and study groups on WhatsApp.
   - Post-exam **"Share Score on WhatsApp"** button with pre-formatted scorecard and invite link.

## 🚀 Quick Start & How to Run

### 1. Start the Local Server
From the project folder, run:
```bash
npm start
# or
node server.js
```

The server automatically detects your network configuration and displays:
- **Local Browser Link**: `http://localhost:3000`
- **Wi-Fi / Hotspot Share Link**: `http://<your-local-ip>:3000` (e.g., `http://192.168.0.102:3000`)

---

## 📲 How to Share the Link on WhatsApp

### Option A: Local Wi-Fi / Hotspot Sharing (Instant, No Setup Required)
If the candidate's phone or laptop is connected to the **same Wi-Fi router or phone mobile hotspot** as your computer:
1. Copy the network link printed in your terminal (e.g., `http://192.168.0.102:3000`).
2. Paste and send it in your WhatsApp chat or group.
3. Anyone who taps the link can immediately open the test on their mobile browser!

### Option B: Worldwide Public Link over the Internet (Free, 1 Command)
To share a public link that anyone in Telangana or anywhere across the world can access over mobile data (4G/5G):

**Using Cloudflare Tunnel (Recommended - Fast & Free):**
```bash
npx cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:3000
```
*Cloudflare will print a secure public link like `https://xyz-random.trycloudflare.com` which you can share directly over WhatsApp!*

**Using Localtunnel:**
```bash
npx localtunnel --port 3000
```

## 📅 How to Create New Daily Mock Tests

To generate a new 100-question mock test for Day 3, Day 4, etc.:
```bash
# Generate Day 3 paper
python3 create_daily_mock.py --day 3

# Or generate a test to a custom path
python3 create_daily_mock.py --day 4 --out public/data/mock_test_4.json
```

Candidates can take any day's test directly by choosing from the dropdown on the home page or by sharing the direct URL parameter on WhatsApp:
- Day 1: `http://<your-link>/?test=mock_test_1.json`
- Day 2: `http://<your-link>/?test=mock_test_2.json`
- Day 3: `http://<your-link>/?test=mock_test_3.json`
